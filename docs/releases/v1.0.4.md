# MeetRec 1.0.4

MeetRec 1.0.4 adds pause and resume for microphone, system audio, and combined recordings.

## Highlights

- Choose **Pause Recording** from the tray or floating timer menu. Both indicators turn yellow and the timer holds its current time.
- Click either yellow indicator or choose **Resume Recording** to continue the same recording. Saved audio and silence auto-stop skip the paused interval.
- Choose **Cancel This Recording** while paused to discard the take. The confirmation opens in front of other windows.

## Artifact

Download `MeetRec-1.0.4-win-x64.zip`, extract the folder, and run `MeetRec\MeetRec.exe`.
